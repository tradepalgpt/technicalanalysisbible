Technical Analysis Bible is a free reference to technical indicators, chart patterns, candlesticks and trading methods.

Most trading sites either list hundreds of indicators in a sentence each, or bury the useful part under sales pitches. We do one thing: every guide answers the same questions, in the same order, in plain English. What is it? How is it calculated? How do you read it? When does it work, and when does it mislead? What mistakes do people make? What does it pair well with?

## Who it is for

Anyone learning technical analysis, and experienced traders who want a quick, reliable check of a formula or default setting.

## How it is paid for

The site is free to read and is supported by advertising, including affiliate links to a broker. Advertisers have no say over what we write. See [how we make money](/advertising-disclosure/).

## Not advice

Everything here is general education. Please read our [risk warning](/risk-warning/) before trading.
