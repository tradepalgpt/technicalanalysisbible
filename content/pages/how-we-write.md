## One shape for every guide

Every guide uses the same sections in the same order: a short answer, an at-a-glance table, what it is, the formula or drawing rules, how to read it, the market states it suits, settings, common mistakes, what it pairs well with, related guides, sources and frequently asked questions.

## Facts we check

Formulas, default settings, the inventor and the year are checked against the original book or paper wherever possible. Each guide lists its sources. If we cannot confirm something, we leave it out rather than guess.

## What we do not do

- We do not claim win rates or profits we cannot source.
- We do not recommend brokers inside guides. Ads are kept separate and labelled.
- We do not show back-test results unless they are clearly labelled, include costs, and explain how they were made.

## How guides are produced

Guides are drafted with the help of AI writing tools using a fixed brief, then checked automatically for structure and banned claims, and fact-checked by a person before publishing. Every guide shows when it was last reviewed.
