Pivot points and Fibonacci retracement both draw horizontal levels where price might react. Pivot points are calculated from the previous period's high, low and close, so everyone gets the same levels. Fibonacci retracement is drawn by hand between a swing low and a swing high, so the levels depend on which swing you pick.

## At a glance

| | Pivot Points | Fibonacci Retracement |
|---|---|---|
| Based on | Previous period high, low and close | A chosen swing low and swing high |
| Levels | P, R1 to R3, S1 to S3 | 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6% |
| Created by | Not attributed to one person; floor traders | No single inventor; ratios from the Fibonacci sequence |
| Subjective? | No, fixed formula | Yes, swing choice varies |
| Best market state | Range | Trend (pullbacks) |
| Main weakness | Weak in strong trends and on big news days | Weak in ranges with no clear swing |

## When to use Pivot Points

[Pivot points](/pivot-points/) suit day traders who want an objective map at the start of the session. Levels are known in advance and identical for everyone using the same data. The central pivot also gives a quick tilt: above it leans bullish, below it bearish.

## When to use Fibonacci Retracement

[Fibonacci retracement](/fibonacci-retracement/) suits traders who plan around pullbacks in a trend. It frames how deep a pullback might go as a share of the prior move. It works on any time frame, though the swing must be chosen with care.

## Using both together

Some traders look for places where a pivot level and a Fibonacci level land close together. This overlap can be treated as a stronger area of interest because more traders may watch it. Treat it as a zone, not an exact price. Add [support and resistance](/support-and-resistance/) for further context. There is also a hybrid, [Fibonacci pivot points](/fibonacci-pivot-points/), which applies Fibonacci ratios to the pivot range.

## Common mix-ups

- **Confusing Fibonacci pivot points with Fibonacci retracement.** The first is a pivot variant; the second is drawn from a swing.
- **Thinking pivot levels are Fibonacci levels.** Standard pivots use simple arithmetic.
- **Expecting exact reversals.** Both give zones where reactions may occur.
- **Picking different swings.** Two people can draw different retracements on the same chart.

## FAQ

**Are pivot points the same as Fibonacci levels?**
No. Standard pivot points come from the previous high, low and close with simple formulas. Fibonacci levels come from ratios applied to a swing.

**Which is better for day trading?**
Neither is better in general. Pivot points are quick and fixed. Fibonacci retracement fits pullbacks. Test them on your market.

**Is the 50% level a Fibonacci ratio?**
No. It is included because of older ideas about half-way retracements.

**Can pivots and Fibonacci be used together?**
Yes. Some traders look for levels that overlap and treat them as a zone of interest.
