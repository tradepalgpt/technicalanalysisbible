Volume Profile and Market Profile both show price action as a distribution across price levels, and both mark a point of control and a value area. Volume Profile counts the volume traded at each price. Market Profile counts the time spent at each price, using letter blocks called TPOs.

## At a glance

| | Volume Profile | Market Profile |
|---|---|---|
| Counts | Volume at each price | Time at each price (TPOs) |
| Origin | Not attributed to one person; related to Market Profile | J. Peter Steidlmayer, Chicago Board of Trade, 1980s |
| Default settings | Session or fixed range; value area 70% of volume | 30-minute periods; value area 70% of TPOs |
| Needs volume data? | Yes | No, only price and time |
| Best market state | Ranges, breakouts | Range, breakout |
| Main weakness | Weak in strong trends away from old high-volume areas | Weak in strong trends, where value keeps moving |

## When to use Volume Profile

[Volume Profile](/volume-profile/) suits traders who want to see where trading actually happened by size. Its [Point of Control](/point-of-control/) is the price with the most volume. It is simple to add to a chart on most platforms and works on any chosen range, not just a day.

## When to use Market Profile

[Market Profile](/market-profile/) suits traders who work with auction market theory and want to see how value develops through the day. Its letter structure shows when price was accepted or rejected. Because it needs only price and time, it works where volume is unreliable.

## Using both together

The two often show similar levels but not always. A price that stands out in both, such as a point of control that matches, is a stronger area of interest than one that appears in only one. A gap between them can show a level where price lingered but little traded. Both describe the past and give no forecast. The [Value Area](/value-area/) is the shared idea.

## Common mix-ups

- **Thinking they are the same.** One uses volume, the other uses time.
- **Assuming the point of control matches.** The two can pick different prices.
- **Expecting exact turns.** Both mark zones of interest, not certain reversals.
- **Using forex tick volume as real volume.** Volume Profile on spot forex is an approximation. See [tick volume](/tick-volume/).

## FAQ

**What is the difference between Volume Profile and Market Profile?**
Volume Profile counts volume traded at each price. Market Profile counts time spent at each price, shown as stacked letters.

**Which one is better?**
Neither is better in general. Volume Profile is more common and easy to read. Market Profile suits auction theory and works without volume data.

**Do they use the same value area?**
Both conventionally use 70%, but of different things: 70% of volume or 70% of TPOs.

**Who created Market Profile?**
J. Peter Steidlmayer developed it at the Chicago Board of Trade, with the approach popularised in the 1980s.
