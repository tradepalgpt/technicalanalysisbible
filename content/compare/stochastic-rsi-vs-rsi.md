Stochastic RSI applies the Stochastic formula to RSI values instead of to price. It shows where the current RSI sits within its own recent range, so it reaches its extremes far more often than RSI does. RSI is smoother and slower. Stochastic RSI is faster and jumpier, so it gives more signals and more false ones.

## At a glance

| | Stochastic RSI | RSI |
|---|---|---|
| Measures | RSI relative to its own recent high-low range | Average gains vs average losses |
| Created by | Tushar Chande and Stanley Kroll, 1994 | J. Welles Wilder Jr., 1978 |
| Default settings | RSI 14, Stochastic 14, %K 3, %D 3 | 14 periods |
| Levels | 0.8 and 0.2 (or 80 and 20) | 70 and 30 |
| Scale | 0 to 1 or 0 to 100, by platform | 0 to 100 |
| Speed | Faster, more signals | Smoother, fewer signals |
| Best market state | Range, reversal | Range, reversal |
| Weak in | Strong trends, noisy markets | Strong trends |

## When to use Stochastic RSI

[Stochastic RSI](/stochastic-rsi/) suits traders who want more frequent entry timing, especially in ranges. RSI can go long periods without reaching 70 or 30 in quiet markets, and Stochastic RSI reaches its extremes often. That makes it useful for timing small turns inside a range, but it also makes it jumpy.

## When to use RSI

[RSI](/rsi/) suits traders who want a steadier reading. Its 50 line works as a simple trend filter, and its divergences are widely watched. It is a good starting point if you want a single momentum gauge across many markets and time frames.

## Using both together

Stochastic RSI is built from RSI, so showing both adds little independent evidence. Some traders use RSI for the bigger momentum view and Stochastic RSI for timing. Agreement between them is weak confirmation, because they share the same source. A tool of a different kind, such as [support and resistance](/support-and-resistance/) or [volume](/volume/), adds more.

## Common mix-ups

- **Using RSI levels on Stochastic RSI.** The usual levels are 80 and 20, or 0.8 and 0.2, not 70 and 30.
- **Mixing up scales.** Some platforms show 0 to 1, others 0 to 100, with smoothing added.
- **Treating overbought as a sell signal.** Both can stay high in a strong trend.
- **Confusing it with the Stochastic Oscillator.** That one applies the formula to price. See [Stochastic Oscillator](/stochastic-oscillator/).

## FAQ

**Is Stochastic RSI better than RSI?**
Neither is better in general. Stochastic RSI is faster and gives more signals, with more noise. RSI is calmer.

**Why does Stochastic RSI hit 0 and 1 so often?**
It measures RSI against its own recent range. When RSI is at its highest of the lookback, it reads 1.

**What are the usual settings?**
RSI 14, Stochastic 14, %K 3 and %D 3, with levels at 0.8 and 0.2.

**Do I need both on the same chart?**
Not usually. One is built from the other.
