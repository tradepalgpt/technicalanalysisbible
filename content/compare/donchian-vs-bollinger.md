Donchian Channels and Bollinger Bands both draw an upper and lower line around price, but they are built differently. Donchian lines mark the highest high and lowest low of the last N bars, so they step with price extremes. Bollinger Bands sit two standard deviations from a moving average, so they widen and narrow smoothly with volatility. One is a breakout tool, the other a volatility envelope.

## At a glance

| | Donchian Channels | Bollinger Bands |
|---|---|---|
| Built from | Highest high and lowest low | Moving average and standard deviation |
| Default settings | 20 periods | 20-period SMA, 2 standard deviations |
| Created by | Richard Donchian, mid-20th century | John Bollinger, early 1980s |
| Shape | Stepped lines | Smooth curves |
| Middle line | Halfway between upper and lower | 20-period SMA |
| Best market state | Breakout, trend | Ranges, volatility changes, breakouts |
| Main use | Breakout entries, trailing exits | Relative high and low, squeeze |
| Weak in | Ranges, where breakouts fail | Strong trends, if used for fading |

## When to use Donchian Channels

[Donchian Channels](/donchian-channels/) suit trend followers. A close above the upper line is a new 20-bar high, and a close below the lower line is a new 20-bar low. The same lines can trail a stop, and the Turtle rules paired a long period for entries with a shorter one for exits. See [Turtle Trading Rules](/turtle-trading/).

## When to use Bollinger Bands

[Bollinger Bands](/bollinger-bands/) suit traders who want to judge whether price is high or low against its recent volatility. In a range, band touches can mark places to look for a turn. The squeeze, when the bands are unusually narrow, shows quiet markets that may be followed by larger moves. It does not show the direction.

## Using both together

Because they measure different things, the pair can give two views. A Donchian breakout that arrives after a Bollinger squeeze combines a new range high with a volatility expansion. Also, in a trend, price can walk the Bollinger band while the Donchian lower line trails behind as a stop. Add [ATR](/atr/) to size stops and [volume](/volume/) to judge a breakout.

## Common mix-ups

- **Treating a band touch as a breakout signal.** For Bollinger Bands, a touch is not a signal on its own.
- **Reading a close outside Bollinger Bands as a Donchian breakout.** The two lines are not equivalent.
- **Forgetting the lookback rule.** Some platforms include the current bar in the Donchian window, others do not.
- **Expecting similar width.** Donchian width is the recent range. Bollinger width is volatility.

## FAQ

**Which is better for breakouts?**
Donchian Channels are built for them, since the line is the recent high or low. Bollinger Bands can mark a breakout after a squeeze.

**Do Donchian Channels use standard deviation?**
No. They use only the highest high and lowest low over the period.

**Do either repaint?**
No. Once a bar closes, its values are fixed. Lines can move during the current bar.

**Can both be used in ranges?**
Bollinger Bands are more suited to ranges. Donchian breakouts tend to fail there and produce many small losses.
