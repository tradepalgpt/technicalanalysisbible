Support is a price area where falling price has repeatedly slowed or turned up, because buyers have tended to step in there.

## Explained

Support is drawn as a horizontal line or a zone under the current price, usually through earlier lows. The more times price bounces from the area, the more traders watch it. A rising [trendline](/trendline/) can also act as support. See [support and resistance](/support-and-resistance/) for how to draw and rank these areas.

Support is not a floor. When price closes clearly below it, the area often becomes resistance, a switch known as [role reversal](/role-reversal/). Each extra test can also weaken a level, since fewer buyers remain waiting there.

## Example

A share falls to 100, bounces to 110, falls back to 100 and bounces again. The 100 area is now treated as support. If price later closes at 97 and then rallies only as far as 100 before dropping again, that old support has turned into resistance.
