An extension is a move that carries price beyond a previous swing high or low, or a tool that projects where such a move might reach.

## Explained

The word has two uses. In ordinary chart reading, an extended move is one that has travelled unusually far from its starting point or from an average, and may be stretched.

As a tool, an extension projects levels beyond a prior swing using ratios. The [Fibonacci Extension](/fibonacci-extension/) places levels at ratios such as 127.2 percent and 161.8 percent of an earlier move. Related tools are [Fibonacci Projection](/fibonacci-projection/) and the [Measured Move](/measured-move/).

Traders often use extension levels as places to consider a [profit target](/profit-target/). They do not always mark turning points.

## Example

Price rises from 100 to 120, pulls back to 110, then rises again. The first swing was 20 points. A 161.8 percent extension of that swing, measured from 110, is 110 plus 32.4, which is 142.4. Some traders watch that level as a target.
