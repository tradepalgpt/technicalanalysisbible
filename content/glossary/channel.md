A channel is a pair of lines, usually parallel, that contain price between an upper boundary and a lower boundary.

## Explained

A channel can slope up, slope down or run flat. In an upward channel, the lower line joins swing lows and the upper line joins swing highs. Price tends to move between the two lines while the channel holds.

Traders use the lines as areas of support and resistance. A close outside the channel suggests the pattern may be ending or speeding up.

Some channels are drawn by hand, as in [Trend Channel](/trend-channel/). Others are calculated, such as [Donchian Channels](/donchian-channels/) and [Keltner Channels](/keltner-channels/). Flat channels are the setting for [Range Trading](/range-trading/).

## Example

A stock makes lows at 40, 43 and 46, and highs at 48, 51 and 54. A line through the lows and a parallel line through the highs form a rising channel about 8 points wide. If price closes below the lower line, the channel is considered broken.
