Delta is the difference between buying volume and selling volume over a period, usually measured as trades at the ask minus trades at the bid.

## Explained

Each trade has a buyer and a seller. Delta classifies a trade by who acted first. A trade at the ask price counts as buying pressure, and a trade at the bid price counts as selling pressure. Positive delta means more aggressive buying, and negative delta means more aggressive selling.

Delta needs data that shows trades at the bid and ask, so it is mostly used on futures and other exchange-traded markets. See [Volume Delta](/volume-delta/) for a full description and [Cumulative Volume Delta](/cumulative-volume-delta/) for the running total. The [Footprint Chart](/footprint-chart/) displays delta inside each price level.

In options, "delta" has a different meaning: how much an option's price changes when the underlying moves.

## Example

In one minute, 600 contracts trade at the ask and 400 trade at the bid. Delta is 600 minus 400, which is +200. A positive number shows buyers were the more aggressive side in that minute.
