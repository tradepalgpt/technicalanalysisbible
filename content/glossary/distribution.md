Distribution is a phase in which large holders gradually sell their positions, usually inside a sideways range near a market top.

## Explained

Distribution is the mirror image of accumulation. Large sellers cannot unload all at once without pushing price down, so they sell in pieces while demand is still strong enough to absorb it. On a chart this often shows as a sideways range after a long rise.

The [Wyckoff Method](/wyckoff-method/) describes the stages in [Wyckoff Distribution](/wyckoff-distribution/). Volume indicators such as [On-Balance Volume](/on-balance-volume/) are used to look for selling inside the range.

Like accumulation, it is usually clear only afterwards. A range at a high can also end with price rising again.

## Example

A stock climbs from 70 to 100, then trades between 95 and 100 for six weeks. Up days show falling volume and down days show rising volume. Some traders read this as distribution. A close below 95 on heavy volume would support that reading.
