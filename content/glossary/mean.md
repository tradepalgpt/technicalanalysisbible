The mean is the average of a set of numbers: add them up and divide by how many there are. In trading it often means the average price over a recent period.

## Explained

A [simple moving average](/simple-moving-average/) is a mean of the last set of closing prices, recalculated on each new bar. Many tools treat it as a centre line. [Bollinger Bands](/bollinger-bands/) place their bands a number of [standard deviations](/standard-deviation/) above and below that mean.

The idea behind [mean reversion](/mean-reversion/) is that price tends to drift back toward its average after stretching away from it. That does not always happen. In a strong trend, price can stay far from its mean for a long time, and the average simply follows it.

## Example

Five closing prices are 10, 12, 11, 13 and 14. Their sum is 60 and there are five of them, so the mean is 12. If price jumps to 16, it is 4 above its mean, which some traders call stretched.
