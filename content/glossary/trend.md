A trend is the general direction price is moving over a chosen period: up when it makes higher highs and higher lows, down when it makes lower highs and lower lows.

## Explained

A trend is defined by structure. In an uptrend, each rally goes higher than the last and each pullback holds above the previous low. In a downtrend it is the reverse. When neither pattern holds, the market is moving sideways in a trading range.

Trends depend on timeframe. A market can be in an uptrend on a daily chart and a downtrend on a five-minute chart. [Dow theory](/dow-theory/) gives the classic description. Tools such as the [trendline](/trendline/) and the [ADX](/adx/) help draw and measure a trend, and [trend following](/trend-following/) is the approach of trading with it.

## Example

A share makes swing lows at 50, 54 and 58, and swing highs at 60, 65 and 70. Each low and high is above the one before, so it is an uptrend. If price then falls to 56, below the last low of 58, the pattern of higher lows breaks and the trend is in doubt.
