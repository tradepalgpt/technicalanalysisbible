## How we make money

- **Affiliate partner ads.** Some ads link to a broker, currently IC Markets. If you open an account through one of those links, we may earn a commission. This costs you nothing extra.
- Partner ads are always marked **"Advertisement"**, sit apart from the article text and carry the provider's own risk warning.
- Partner links are marked to search engines as sponsored (`rel="sponsored"`).

## What advertising does not change

- Advertisers do not write, review or approve our content.
- The article text stays neutral and does not recommend any broker.
- Showing an ad is not an endorsement or a recommendation of that firm or its products. Check that any firm is regulated where you live before you use it.

## Where ads are not shown

We do not show broker ads to visitors in some countries, including the United Kingdom, where rules on financial promotions apply.
