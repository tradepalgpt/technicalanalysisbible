This policy explains what {{BRAND}} ({{DOMAIN}}) collects when you visit.

## What we collect

- **No accounts, no forms, no comments.** We do not ask for your name or email.
- **Cookie-free analytics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.
- **Server logs.** Our host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. We use your approximate country (not your IP address) only to decide whether to show a partner ad.
- **Partner ads.** Ad images load from the advertiser's servers, which will see your IP address and browser details. If you click an ad, the advertiser's own privacy policy applies from then on, and it may use a tracking link to credit us.

## What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, build profiles about you, or use advertising cookies. If that changes (for example if we add a display ad network), we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK and EU GDPR), you can ask what data we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Because we collect almost nothing that identifies you, there is usually nothing to return. Contact: {{CONTACT}}.

You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).
