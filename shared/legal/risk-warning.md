## What you should know before trading

- **CFDs, spread bets, forex and futures are leveraged.** A small price move can cause a large loss. CFDs are complex instruments and come with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. Most retail CFD accounts lose money. You should consider whether you understand how CFDs work and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money.
- **Indicators and chart patterns describe past prices.** They do not predict the future. No indicator, pattern or method on this site works all the time, and many fail often.
- **Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.** Any example, chart or test shown here is for illustration only. Hypothetical or back-tested results have limits: they are made with hindsight and do not include all real-world costs such as spreads, commissions, slippage, swaps and gaps.
- **Only trade with money you can afford to lose.** If you are unsure, speak to an independent, authorised financial adviser in your country.
